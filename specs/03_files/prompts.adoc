= 03 Files: prompts

The prompts used in this lesson, in order, cleaned up so they can be replayed as-is.

== 1. Floor plans

=== 1.1 Spec

[source,text]
----
Write a spec specs/03_files/01-floor-plan.spec.adoc: each floor has a plan (an image)
that the office manager uploads in the floor form; the employee sees the plan of the chosen
room's or desk's floor right in the booking form, with a download button. PNG and JPEG only,
up to 10 MB. Where the files live in dev - also into the spec. Roles and the container are
not part of this step.
----

Result: the spec, with the storage location read from the Jmix defaults:
`<project root>/.jmix/work/filestorage/<yyyy>/<MM>/<dd>/<uuid>.<ext>`.

=== 1.2 Decisions and implementation

[source,text]
----
1 - the 10 MB limit on the upload field only; 2 - check the file name, not the content;
3 - keep the preview in the floor form. Implement.
----

Result: `Floor.plan` (`FileRef`), the upload field and preview, a saving listener for the
PNG/JPEG rule with `FloorPlanValidationTest`, and the floor-plan section with download in
the booking form.
